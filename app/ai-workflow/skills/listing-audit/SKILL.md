---
name: listing-audit
description: Focused visual and interaction QA workflow for desktop vacation-rental listing submissions.
---

1. Capture the listing at 1440px and compare content rail, header, title, hero tile geometry, and card alignment to approved reference evidence.
2. Open the photo tour from both the primary image and “Show all photos”; verify close and focus restoration.
3. Open the lightbox from a photo-tour image; test button navigation, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Escape, image alt text, and the photo counter.
4. Test with reduced motion enabled and keyboard-only navigation.
5. Record concrete deviations rather than claiming unverified pixel perfection.
